Getting there

Birch Hills Airport is on Highway 3, about 25 minutes from Prince Albert and 1.5 hours from Saskatoon. Directions from both are linked in the schedule's left-hand column.

Carpooling is often available from Saskatoon, Martensville and Warman. Ask in the WhatsApp group.

PAGSC is a non-profit club affiliated with the Soaring Association of Saskatchewan and the Soaring Association of Canada. This is a friendly guide. The schedule sheet, pagsc.ca and our instructors are the final word on dates, weather and fees. Prices current at October 2026.
