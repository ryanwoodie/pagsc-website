Where you type. You only ever use one column (your day) and one block (your sign-up section). Ignore the rest.
1

Open the schedule

On a computer, open the link in your browser. Sign in with any Google account.

2

Find your day

Dates run across the top row. Scroll sideways until yours is on screen. Weekends are the main flying days.

3

Find your block

First flight: the cell reading “Intro Fam Flight Sign-up Below”. Lesson: “Student/Pilots Sign-up Below”.

4

Type your name

Use the first empty cell underneath and press Enter. For a first flight, add your phone number. It saves by itself.

On a phone?
In a phone browser the sheet is usually view-only. Install the free Google Sheets app and open the link there.
