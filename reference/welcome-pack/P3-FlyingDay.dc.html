Sign up early, even as a maybe

Write “(maybe)” after your name. It helps instructors plan the day. If your plans change, delete your name.

Your first day

Arrive at the time posted for your date in the Status / Comments row. Arrival times change with the season and conditions, so have a quick look that morning.

Bring
  • Hat, sunscreen, sunglasses
  • Water, lunch or snacks
  • Layers: it's cooler at altitude
  • A way to pay by e-transfer
  • A phone or camera, if you like
How the day runs
  1. Meet the crew and get a safety briefing.
  2. Help get the glider out. Pitching in is part of the fun, and it keeps costs low.
  3. Fly: a winch launch to about 2,000 feet, with a club pilot beside you the whole time.
  4. Stay, watch, learn, and fly again if time allows.

Guest flying is usually on weekends and holidays, about 1 to 5 pm, weather permitting. The main season runs spring through fall, with occasional winter flying.
