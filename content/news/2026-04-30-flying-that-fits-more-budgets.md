Located in Birch Hills, Saskatchewan, our rates cater to people from many backgrounds and interests in flying: whether you are looking to cross something off a bucket list, or start a journey in soaring, we are here to support you.

A Discovery Flight is $50, or $40 each for a group of four or more. Membership is $150 a year for regular members, $75 for juniors, and $15 for youth and Air Cadets, and every membership includes the Soaring Association of Canada. Once you are a member, a winch launch is $5, the glider rents for $30 an hour, and instruction is free. A typical 10-minute lesson flight costs about $10.

See the full breakdown on our [Costs](https://www.pagsc.ca/costs/) page.
