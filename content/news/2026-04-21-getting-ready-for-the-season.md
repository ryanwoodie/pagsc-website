Spring is on the horizon (or at least, we hope it is), and it is time to start thinking about how you will spend the warm season.

Are you ready to take to the skies and challenge yourself? The club flies from Birch Hills Airport, about 25 minutes southeast of Prince Albert. Guest flights and lessons run on weekends and holidays, about 11 am to 5 pm, weather permitting.

Come check out the Prince Albert Gliding and Soaring Club this season. A [Discovery Flight](https://www.pagsc.ca/discovery-flight/) is $50, and no experience is needed.
