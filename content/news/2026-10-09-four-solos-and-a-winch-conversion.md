It has been a busy month at Birch Hills. Four pilots flew their first glider solos. Jannie Kemp flew his first solo on September 6, Preston and Grant soloed in the past month as well, and Jason Savage flew his first solo on Sunday, October 4.

Stephen, from the Saskatoon Soaring Club, also completed his winch conversion with the club. A winch conversion trains a pilot who has flown gliders before to launch by winch, the way flights at Birch Hills begin.

A first solo is the milestone every student pilot works toward: the first time you take a glider up entirely on your own, with everything you have learned in dual lessons behind you. Congratulations to all five pilots.
