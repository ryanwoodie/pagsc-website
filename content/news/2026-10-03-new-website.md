The club has a new home at pagsc.ca. You can now buy a Discovery Flight online, for yourself or as a gift, and pick a day and time on the booking calendar. Weekend spots are pencilled in straight away; weekday flying is on request.

The home page shows the next flying days from the club's Flying Schedule, with the latest status from the field. Members still book lessons on the schedule itself, and the Members page has the walkthrough.

There is more for anyone thinking of learning to fly: what the first visits look like, what a licence costs, and the path from first lesson to solo.
