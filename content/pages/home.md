# Come fly with us.

No engine. No experience needed. A winch launch to about 1,500 feet, and some of the quietest flying in Saskatchewan.

## Three facts

- **$50** first flight, no membership needed
- **Free** instruction from club instructors
- **25 min** from Prince Albert, 1.5 hours from Saskatoon

## How it works

### 1. Buy your flight
$50 online, for yourself or as a gift.

### 2. Book your flying day
Pick a day and time on our booking calendar.

### 3. Come out and fly
Meet the crew at Birch Hills Airport, get a safety briefing, and launch.

## Choose your path

### Try a flight
A Discovery Flight with a club pilot. $50.

### Learn to fly
Free instruction. About $10 an instructional flight for members.

### Youth and cadets
$15 membership and $1,000 in bursaries. Solo at 14.

### Power pilots
A glider licence in 3 flight hours, with ground school and the exam waived.

## Why gliding

### A sport you never finish

Gliding is to powered flying what sailing is to motorboating. With an engine, the flying serves the trip. In a glider, the flying is the game.

[Why gliding]

## What it costs

**$10** is about what a typical 10-minute instructional flight costs members: a $5 launch plus $5 of glider time. Instruction is free.

## The club

A friendly non-profit club, founded in 1986 and affiliated with the Soaring Association of Saskatchewan and the Soaring Association of Canada. The longest cross-country flight out of PAGSC is 632 km.
