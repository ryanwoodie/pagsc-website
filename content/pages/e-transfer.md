# Pay by e-transfer

Choose how many flights and we give you a reference. Send the e-transfer with the reference in the message, and we email your certificates when it arrives. Then pick your day on the calendar.

## Your flights

How many flights? ($50 each, or $40 each for 4 or more) · Your name · Email ("We email your certificates here.") · Name on the certificate (if it's a gift) · Message on the certificate (optional)

When it arrives, we email your certificates, each with a booking code. Then pick your flying day on the calendar. Certificates never expire.

Payment option: "I'll pay now by e-transfer ($50)". After booking: "Pay by e-transfer. Send $50 to … Put this in the message: ET-…. Please send it by Friday, October 16 at 10:30 am, or the spot is released. We email you when it arrives."
