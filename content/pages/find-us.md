# Find us

We fly from **Birch Hills Airport**, on Highway 3.

- **25 minutes** from Prince Albert
- **1.5 hours** from Saskatoon

Map: Birch Hills Airport, Saskatchewan

[Directions from Prince Albert] · [Directions from Saskatoon]

## Carpooling

Carpooling is often available from Saskatoon, Martensville and Warman. Ask when you request your flight.

## When we fly

Weekends and holidays, about 11 am to 5 pm for guest flights, weather permitting. Some weekdays too, often on request. The main season runs spring through fall, with occasional winter flying.

## On the day

Arrive at the time we give you when we confirm your date. Arrival times change with the season and conditions, so have a quick look at your confirmation that morning.
