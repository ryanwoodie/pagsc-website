# Learning to fly

Instruction is free. You pay for launches and glider time: about $10 for a typical instructional flight.

- **Any age** to start training, no experience needed
- **Age 14** to fly solo
- **Age 16** to hold a full Glider Pilot Licence

## What it costs

**$10** is about what a typical 10-minute instructional flight costs: a $5 launch plus $5 of glider time.

Membership is $150 a year, $75 for juniors and $15 for youth and Air Cadets.

## Your path to a Glider Pilot Licence

### 1. Flight lessons
Dual lessons in the air with an instructor, at your own pace. Book each one on the schedule.

### 2. Medical: do this early
Most students use a Category 4 medical, a self-declaration with no doctor's visit. Do it and your student licence paperwork early. A Category 1 or 3 also counts.

### 3. Ground school and the GLIDE exam
15 hours, self-directed or with pilottraining.ca, then Transport Canada's GLIDE written exam (60% to pass).

### 4. First solo, from age 14
Once your instructor is satisfied you are ready.

### 5. Licence, from age 16
Solo practice, then a flight test. You need at least 6 hours of flight time, including 1 hour dual and 2 hours solo.

## Who can learn

- All flying before solo is dual, with a certified instructor as pilot-in-command.
- You need to reach the controls comfortably (roughly 5 ft tall) and be ready to learn (typically 12 and up).
- Maximum weight for an introductory flight is 230 lbs.
- Our two-seat trainer is a Schleicher K7.

## Come regularly if you can

Skills fade between visits. Flying as many weekends (and even weekdays) as you can over a stretch of the season gets you solo sooner and costs less in total.

## Two fast tracks

### Youth and cadets
$15 membership and $1,000 in bursaries toward launches and glider rental.

### Power pilots
With a PPL(A), ground school and the written exam are waived, and you need 3 flight hours, not 6.

## Learn at home, free

- **SOAR manual:** a free PDF from the Soaring Association of Canada
- **Safe winch launching videos:** British Gliding Association
- **Glider Flying Handbook:** free from the FAA
