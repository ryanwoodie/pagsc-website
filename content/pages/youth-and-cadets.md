# Youth and cadets: learn for less

**$1,000** toward launches and glider rental: a $500 Soaring Association of Canada bursary, matched by $500 from the club.

With the $15 youth membership, a glider licence can cost very little. Ask us and we'll help you apply.

[Book a first lesson]

## For parents

- All flying before solo is dual, with a certified instructor as pilot-in-command.
- Training can start at any age. Students need to reach the controls comfortably (roughly 5 ft tall) and be ready to learn (typically 12 and up).
- Solo is possible from age 14, once the instructor is satisfied the student is ready.
- A full Glider Pilot Licence can be held from age 16.
- Carpooling is often available from Saskatoon, Martensville and Warman.

## Air Cadets

Air Cadets who earn their Glider Pilot Licence here can also do a Soaring Pilot Conversion for their cadet wings.

## What it costs

- Youth and Air Cadet membership: $15 a year, for members under 19
- Winch launch: $5
- Glider time: $0.50 a minute
- Instruction: free

## The path

The same five steps as every student: flight lessons, medical, ground school and the GLIDE exam, first solo, licence.
