# Members

## The Flying Schedule, made easy

Booking means typing your name into one cell of a shared Google Sheet. It takes about a minute.

Drawing: the map of the sheet from page 2 of the welcome pack.

Caption: Where you type. You only ever use one column (your day) and one block (your sign-up section).

### 1. Open the schedule
On a computer, open the link in your browser. Sign in with any Google account.

### 2. Find your day
Dates run across the top row. Scroll sideways until yours is on screen. Weekends are the main flying days.

### 3. Find your block
For a lesson, look for the cell reading "Student/Pilots Sign-up Below". For a guest's first flight: "Intro Fam Flight Sign-up Below".

### 4. Type your name
Use the first empty cell underneath and press Enter. It saves by itself.

**On a phone?** In a phone browser the sheet is usually view-only. Install the free Google Sheets app and open the link there.

## The morning you fly: three checks

- **Status / Comments row:** is flying on, delayed or cancelled? The day's arrival time is posted here too.
- **Weather row:** a quick look at wind and conditions. The forecast fills in about a week ahead.
- **Instructors block:** for lessons, an instructor's name under your date means instruction is on.

Always check the sheet before you leave home. Gliding depends on the weather, and last-minute changes are posted there.

## Sign up early, even as a maybe

Write "(maybe)" after your name. It helps instructors plan the day. If your plans change, delete your name.
